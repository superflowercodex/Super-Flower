The Codex

The Super Flower Cannabis Codex is a living cannabis classification system designed to catalogue, compare, and explore cannabis genetics through standardized specimen profiles.

Each specimen is assigned a unique Codex entry and evaluated across multiple attributes, creating a structured reference system for collectors, enthusiasts, patients, cultivators, and competitors.

Unlike traditional strain lists, the Codex focuses on measurable characteristics and specimen-based comparisons, allowing users to discover, document, and explore cannabis in a consistent and organized format.

Data and Images

Specimen records on the search page are drawn from CannaLib through the Super Flower Engine. Where a value is not held in the record, the Codex may fill it from a web search or calculate an estimate, and each value is labelled by where it came from.

When a specimen has no photograph in its record, the Codex may show an openly licensed reference photograph of cannabis flower from Wikimedia Commons or Openverse. These are labelled as reference photographs, are not verified as that exact strain, and carry their credit. If no suitable photograph is found, an archival illustration is shown instead.

Privacy Policy

The Super Flower Cannabis Codex website does not intentionally collect personal information from visitors.
